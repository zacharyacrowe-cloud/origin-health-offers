Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Origin Health & Wellness (“we,” “us”) handles information on this landing page and when you book an appointment through it.

Information collected on this page

This page does not have its own forms and does not use advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and the time of your visit) in server logs for security and reliability.

Online booking through Sked

When you tap “Book My $49 Visit” or use the scheduler on this page, you are using an online scheduling service provided by Sked, a third-party practice management company. To book, the scheduler may ask for information such as your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, preferred appointment time, and the reason for your visit. That information is collected and stored by Sked on our behalf so we can schedule and prepare for your appointment. Sked’s handling of your information is also governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and remind you about your appointment
  • To contact you about your visit or answer your questions
  • To provide care if you become a patient

Health information you share as a patient is handled in accordance with applicable law, including HIPAA, and our Notice of Privacy Practices, available at our office.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate our practice (such as our scheduling software), or when required by law.

Advertising

You may have reached this page from an ad on Facebook or Instagram. Information you provide on Meta’s platforms is governed by Meta’s privacy policy. This page does not send your booking information back to Meta.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or correct your contact information, or stop sending appointment reminders, by contacting us using the information below.
