Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how Origin Health & Wellness (“we,” “us”) handles information on these offer pages for our Kingston Springs and Nashville offices, and when you book, call, or text us from them.

Information collected on these pages

These pages do not have their own sign-up forms. Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically record basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, and the time of your visit, for security and reliability.

Meta (Facebook/Instagram) pixel

These pages use the Meta pixel, a small piece of code from Meta Platforms, to help us measure how well our Facebook and Instagram ads work. The pixel may record that you viewed a page and that you tapped buttons such as “Book,” “Call,” or “Text,” or reached the scheduler section. Meta may use cookies and similar technologies, along with information it already has about you, to provide this measurement. Meta’s use of this information is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

The pixel does not receive what you type into the booking scheduler, such as your name, contact details, or reason for visit.

Online booking through third-party schedulers

Booking on these pages is handled by third-party scheduling services that work on our behalf:

  • Kingston Springs: Sked (Sked privacy policy)
  • Nashville: ChiroHD (ChiroHD privacy policy)

To book, the scheduler may ask for information such as your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, preferred appointment time, and reason for your visit. That information is collected and stored by the scheduling service so we can schedule and prepare for your appointment. Each service also has its own privacy policy and terms.

Calling or texting us

The Call and Text buttons open your phone’s own calling or messaging app. Calls and texts go directly to our office. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and remind you about your appointment
  • To contact you about your visit or answer your questions
  • To provide care if you become a patient
  • To understand which of our ads and pages are working

Health information you share as a patient is handled in accordance with applicable law, including HIPAA, and our Notice of Privacy Practices, available at our offices.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our practice, such as our scheduling software, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or correct your contact information, or to stop sending appointment reminders, by contacting either office below.

More information
