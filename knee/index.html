Frequently asked questions

What’s included for $49?

A health history, knee exam, X-rays, your first treatment, and an X-ray review where we explain your non-surgical care options.

How long does the visit take?

Plan on about an hour. Exact times can vary depending on whether X-rays are needed.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed. You can book directly online or by calling the office.

I’ve already been told I need a knee replacement. Should I still come in?

Many people want to understand all of their options before deciding on surgery. The assessment gives you more information; it doesn’t replace your surgeon’s advice, and the decision is always yours.

Is treatment included?

Yes — your first treatment is included in the $49 visit. If ongoing care is recommended, we’ll explain your options and costs up front before anything is done.
