Frequently asked questions

What’s included for $49?

A consultation, a full chiropractic exam, X-rays if the doctor determines they’re needed, and a report of findings where we explain what we found and your care options.

How long does the visit take?

Plan on about an hour for your first visit. Exact times can vary depending on whether X-rays are needed.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed. You can book directly online or by calling the office.

Is treatment included?

The $49 special covers your evaluation and report of findings. If care is recommended, we’ll explain your options and costs up front before anything is done.

Can I use insurance?

The $49 special is a flat cash price. Call the office if you have questions about using insurance for ongoing care.

Why only 20 spots?

We limit the number of new patient specials each month so every new patient gets unhurried time with the doctor.
