Frequently asked questions

What’s included for $79?

Your consultation, knee exam, X-rays, decompression, Stemwave shockwave therapy, and a report of findings with your care plan. It’s a complete first visit, not just a screening.

What is Stemwave?

Stemwave is a non-invasive acoustic (shockwave) therapy applied to the area that hurts. There are no needles or medications, and many patients feel relief after a session.

Will I get decompression and Stemwave on my first visit?

Yes, as long as the exam shows they’re appropriate for you. If something in your exam or X-rays means another approach is better, we’ll explain why.

How long does the visit take?

Plan on about an hour to an hour and a half for your first visit.

I’ve already been told I need a knee replacement. Should I still come in?

Many people want to understand all of their options before deciding on surgery. The visit gives you more information; it doesn’t replace your surgeon’s advice, and the decision is always yours.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed. You can book online or by calling the office.

Can I use insurance?

The $79 first visit is a flat cash price. Call the office if you have questions about using insurance for ongoing care.
